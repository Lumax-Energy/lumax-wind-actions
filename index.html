How the coordinates work

South is entered as a positive number of degrees. The map is treated as linear in degrees between its grid lines, so a site's position on the image is:

x = 114.5 + (E − 18) · 687.5 / 14     (pixels, image 958 wide)
y =  58.0 + (S − 22) · 588.5 / 12     (pixels, image 768 high)

Accepted range: E 16.4 – 33.0, S 22.0 – 34.9

When a municipality is selected, vb,0 is its value from the municipality table and the site is put at the municipality's centroid. Otherwise the zone colour under the site is read. Where two zones meet, the higher value is used and a warning is shown. If there is no zone under the site, it falls back to 36 m/s.

Municipality points are centroids. Large or irregular municipalities can have a centroid that falls in a neighbouring zone, so the table value and the colour under the point can differ. For 9 of the 235 municipalities they do.
